Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026 · Last updated: November 18, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how DinunzioDotDev handles information in connection with its public website. We aim to collect only information that is necessary and to describe our practices clearly.

2. Organization Information

DinunzioDotDev is an independent game development studio founded and operated by Anthony Tyler-Scott Dinunzio. This policy applies to dinunziodotdev.ca and its public web pages.

3. Information We Collect

The current website is a public informational site. It does not intentionally collect account information or accept submissions through a form. Basic server and hosting providers may process technical information necessary to deliver and secure the site, such as IP address, requested resource, browser information, and access time.

4. How Information Is Used

Any technical information processed by the hosting or security infrastructure may be used to deliver pages, protect the site, diagnose errors, prevent abuse, and maintain service availability.

5. Cookies and Website Analytics

At present, this website does not intentionally set analytics or advertising cookies. If optional analytics or persistent cookies are added later, this policy will be updated before publication of those technologies.

6. Third-Party Services

The website may rely on hosting, domain, content delivery, or security providers. Those providers process only the information needed to provide their services. Individual games may use third-party services with separate data practices; this website policy does not override those services' terms or policies.

7. Data Sharing and Disclosure

DinunzioDotDev does not sell personal information. Information may be disclosed to service providers supporting hosting or security, or when legally required, authorized, or necessary to protect users and systems.

8. Data Retention

Information is retained only for the period needed for website operation, security, troubleshooting, or legal obligations. Retention periods for hosting and security logs are determined by the applicable service provider.

9. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures may be used to protect website systems. However, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

10. User Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, users may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information that is held by DinunzioDotDev. Users may also withdraw consent or raise privacy concerns where applicable.

11. Children's Privacy

This public website is not directed toward collecting personal information from children. DinunzioDotDev does not knowingly collect personal information from children through this website. Any child-related account or game data practice would be described separately by the applicable game or service.

12. International Data Transfers

Hosting and service providers may process information in Canada or another jurisdiction. Where information leaves a jurisdiction with different privacy protections, reasonable contractual, organizational, or technical safeguards may be used.

13. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated to reflect website changes or legal requirements. The last-updated date above will be revised when a material change is made.

14. Contact Information

Privacy, data protection, and general questions may be directed to anthonydinunziopr@gmail.com or through the Contact page.
