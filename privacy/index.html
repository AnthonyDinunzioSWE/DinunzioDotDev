Privacy policy

This policy describes how Anthony Tyler-Scott Dinunzio, operating as DinunzioDotDev, may collect, use, disclose, and protect personal information in connection with products and projects he creates, develops, publishes, or operates, including software, websites, applications, games, and related services. The information involved depends on the particular product and how you use it; not every product uses every practice or handles every category described here.

Effective date: November 17, 2026

Who is responsible and what this policy covers

Anthony Tyler-Scott Dinunzio, operating as DinunzioDotDev and based in Edmonton, Alberta, Canada, is responsible for personal information he handles in connection with his products and projects, except where another organization is responsible under its own privacy policy or applicable law.

This policy applies to information handled through products and projects created, developed, published, or operated by him, and to related support and communications. A product may provide additional privacy information or choices; if it does, read that information together with this policy. This policy does not cover third-party products, platforms, or services that are not controlled by him.

Privacy questions or requests: contact@dinunziodotdev.ca.

Information that may be collected

Depending on the product, its features, and your choices, information may be provided by you, collected from your device or use of a service, or received from a platform or other source. Categories may include:

  • contact and account information, such as your name, email address, username, or an identifier provided by a sign-in or distribution platform;
  • information you choose to provide, such as messages, support requests, feedback, submissions, or other content;
  • transaction and subscription details, where a product offers paid goods or services (payment credentials are generally handled by the relevant payment provider);
  • usage and interaction information, such as features used, preferences, settings, and activity or diagnostic events;
  • device and technical information, such as browser or device type, operating system, approximate region, IP address, product version, log data, and error or crash reports; and
  • location or other sensitive information only where a product uses it, you provide or authorize it, and collection is permitted by law.

Some information is necessary to provide a requested feature. If you choose not to provide it, that feature may not be available. A product does not collect information for features it does not offer or that are not enabled.

How information is used

Information may be used, as appropriate to the product, to provide and maintain features; create or manage accounts; process transactions; personalize settings or experiences; respond to questions and support requests; understand usage and improve functionality; diagnose problems; protect users, products, and systems; prevent fraud or misuse; and comply with legal obligations or enforce applicable terms.

Where required by applicable privacy law, information is processed on an appropriate legal basis, such as providing a requested service, your consent, legitimate interests, or compliance with a legal obligation. Where consent is the basis, you may withdraw it as permitted by law.

Cookies and similar technologies

Websites or online services may use cookies, local storage, pixels, or similar technologies to remember preferences, support essential functions, measure usage, or, where enabled, support communications or advertising. Available choices may include product settings, consent tools, device controls, or browser settings. Disabling certain technologies may affect functionality. Any third-party analytics or advertising provider used by a product may have its own privacy policy.

How information may be disclosed

Information may be disclosed as needed to provide a product or at your direction, including to service providers that support hosting, storage, communications, analytics, payments, customer support, security, or other operations. Depending on the product and your choices, information may also be shared with platforms or third parties whose features you use, or with advertising or analytics partners.

Information may also be disclosed to comply with law or legal process, respond to valid requests, protect rights and safety, investigate suspected misuse, or in connection with a business transfer. Service providers are authorized to handle information for the services they provide, subject to applicable requirements. Some laws may treat certain disclosures, including for targeted advertising, as a “sale” or “sharing” and provide a right to opt out; where applicable, choices may be available through the product or by contacting the developer.

Retention and security

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including providing the product, resolving disputes, maintaining appropriate records, and meeting legal requirements. Retention periods depend on the type of information, the product, and applicable law. Information may then be deleted, anonymized, or securely stored where deletion is not immediately practicable.

Reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards are used to protect information under the developer's control. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on where you live and subject to applicable law, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of personal information; withdraw consent; object to or restrict certain processing; or opt out of certain targeted advertising, sale, or sharing. You may also have the right to complain to a privacy regulator. Rights and exceptions vary by location.

To make a request, email contact@dinunziodotdev.ca. Identify the product or project and the request, but do not include passwords or unnecessary sensitive information. The developer may need to verify your identity and will respond as required by applicable law. You may stop using a product or adjust available product, device, or browser settings at any time. Information controlled by a third-party platform or provider may need to be managed through that provider.

Children

Products are not intended for children where their use or collection of personal information would be prohibited by applicable law. Age requirements and parental consent rules vary by product and location. If you believe a child has provided personal information in a way that violates applicable requirements, contact the developer. The information will be reviewed and, where appropriate, deleted or handled as required by law.

International processing

The developer and service providers may process or store information in countries other than the country where you live. Privacy laws in those countries may differ. Where required by law, appropriate measures are used for international transfers.

Automated processing

Some products or service providers may use automated tools to support functions such as recommendations, security, moderation, or fraud prevention. Where applicable law grants rights regarding decisions made solely by automated processing that have significant effects, you may contact the developer to ask about those rights.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as products, practices, or legal requirements change. The latest version and its effective date will be posted on this page. Where required, notice of material changes will be provided through an appropriate channel.
