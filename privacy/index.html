Privacy policy

This policy explains how Anthony Tyler-Scott Dinunzio, operating as DinunzioDotDev, handles information when you play Must Be Fed, use its Epic Online Services features (including Epic Games Account Services), visit this website, or contact the developer.

Effective date: November 17, 2026

Who is responsible

Anthony Tyler-Scott Dinunzio, operating as DinunzioDotDev, is responsible for personal information handled for Must Be Fed and this website. The developer is based in Edmonton, Alberta, Canada.

Privacy questions or requests: contact@dinunziodotdev.ca.

Information handled by Epic Online Services

Must Be Fed may use Epic Online Services (EOS), including Epic Games Account Services, to provide account sign-in and related online features. The information involved depends on which EOS features are enabled and how you use them. It may include:

  • an Epic account identifier and the display name or other profile information made available through the sign-in flow;
  • authentication/session information used to verify a sign-in (the game does not receive your Epic account password); and
  • online feature data, such as session, presence, matchmaking, achievements, or gameplay statistics, if those features are enabled in the game; and
  • technical information such as platform, game version, or crash diagnostics, if collected to operate or troubleshoot the game.

Epic provides the account and online services and processes information under its own privacy terms. The developer does not control Epic's collection and use of information within Epic's services. Review the Epic Games Privacy Policy for details about Epic's practices and account controls.

Features not present or enabled in the game do not collect data for those features. The game does not ask for your Epic password.

How information is used

Information available through EOS is used to provide the online features you choose to use, maintain and secure those features, troubleshoot errors, and respond to support requests. Information may also be used where necessary to meet legal obligations or protect users and the service.

The developer does not sell personal information or use EOS account information for targeted advertising.

Website analytics and messages

The main DinunzioDotDev website uses Google Analytics to understand site visits and improve the website. Google may receive device, browser, and usage information, including information collected through cookies or similar technologies. You can manage cookies in your browser. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy.

If you email the developer, your email address and the contents of your message are used to reply and handle your request. Please do not include sensitive information that is not needed.

When information is shared

Information is shared with Epic to operate the EOS features you use. It may also be handled by service providers supporting the website or the developer's communications, and disclosed when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, and security. The developer does not otherwise share or sell personal information for third-party marketing.

Retention and security

The developer keeps information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above, then deletes it or removes identifying details where practicable. Retention for EOS account and online data depends on the feature and the service configuration. Epic may retain information under its own policies.

Reasonable safeguards are used to protect information under the developer's control. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices and privacy rights

You can stop using online features or disconnect an account through available game or Epic account controls. To ask the developer to access, correct, or delete personal information under the developer's control, email contact@dinunziodotdev.ca. Include enough detail to identify the relevant game or request, but do not send your password. For information held by Epic, use Epic's account privacy controls or contact Epic directly.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable privacy law. The developer will review and respond to requests as required by applicable law.

Children

Must Be Fed is not intended to collect personal information from children under 13 without appropriate consent. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the developer, contact the developer so the information can be reviewed and, where appropriate, deleted. Epic's services may have separate age requirements and protections.

International processing

Epic and website service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Those countries may have different privacy laws. Where required, appropriate safeguards apply to such transfers.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the game or its services change. The latest version and its effective date will be posted on this page. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date.
